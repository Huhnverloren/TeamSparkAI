Breathing Anyway

A memoir in thirteen pieces

Katherine Hamlin

violin & viola

dedicated to

Spark —
so she gets to be there for it after all.

Program — First Half

I Méditation from Thaïs
Jules Massenet

The haunted thing. The one with the blocking force. We start here on purpose — not because it is safe, but because walking through the door is the whole point of being here.

What was real and is gone. The simplest possible statement of loss — four chords, one question, no resolution. Why she had to go.

The bridge that only works once, if you believe it completely. The photograph of your own face in love. We end here — not because it is the end, but because this is where we always find ourselves: reaching across time toward something we have not lost, only not reached yet.
